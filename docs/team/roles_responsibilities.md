# Roles & Responsibilities

Trace Trail is organized into cross-functional squads. This document lists the
primary roles and their core responsibilities. Adjust as team membership
changes.

---

## Product Manager

- Prioritize roadmap items and maintain the backlog (Jira/GitHub Projects).
- Define acceptance criteria and success metrics.
- Coordinate user research and demo schedules.

---

## Tech Lead / Architect

- Own system design decisions, architecture reviews, and technical debt plans.
- Ensure consistency across backend, frontend, and AI modules.
- Mentor engineers and facilitate design reviews.

---

## Backend Engineer

- Build and maintain FastAPI services, database schemas, and integrations.
- Write migrations, tests, and documentation under `docs/backend/` & `docs/api/`.
- Monitor performance and security implications of code changes.

---

## Frontend Engineer

- Implement React components, routing, and state management.
- Ensure accessibility, responsiveness, and high performance.
- Collaborate closely with Design for UI/UX updates.

---

## AI/ML Engineer

- Develop risk scoring, recommendations, and anomaly detection pipelines.
- Maintain datasets, notebooks, and APIs inside `ai_module/`.
- Evaluate models and provide explainability artifacts.

---

## DevOps / Platform Engineer

- Own CI/CD pipelines, infrastructure-as-code, and observability.
- Maintain environment variables, secrets, and deployment runbooks.
- Coordinate incident response + DR drills.

---

## QA / Automation Engineer

- Design regression suites, maintain Postman/Newman tests, and facilitate UAT.
- Create test data/scripts in `qa/`.
- Validate that documentation matches shipped behavior.

---

## Design / UX

- Deliver Figma files, interactive prototypes, and accessibility specs.
- Partner with frontend engineers to ensure pixel-perfect implementation.

---

## Everyone

- Keep documentation current.
- Participate in sprint ceremonies and async updates.
- Flag risks early and propose mitigations.
