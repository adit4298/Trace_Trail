# Challenges Guide

Trace Trail challenges turn privacy best practices into achievable milestones.
Use this guide to get the most out of the gamification system.

---

## Challenge Types

| Type            | Description                                      | Example                               |
| --------------- | ------------------------------------------------ | ------------------------------------- |
| Starter         | Quick wins for new users                         | “Secure your passwords”               |
| Platform-specific | Focused on one network                         | “Lock down Instagram privacy”         |
| Habit-building  | Recurring tasks to sustain good hygiene          | “Monthly permission review”           |
| Advanced        | Multi-step initiatives tied to higher point totals| “Full digital footprint audit”       |

---

## Joining a Challenge

1. Navigate to the **Challenges** page.
2. Click a challenge card to view details (description, steps, rewards).
3. Press **Accept Challenge**. The progress bar appears at the top of the page.

---

## Tracking Progress

- Each step corresponds to a recommendation or action (e.g. “Enable 2FA”).
- Completing the associated recommendation automatically advances the challenge.
- Manual actions can be marked complete via the challenge modal.

---

## Rewards & Badges

- Earn points for each step. Points contribute to your overall level.
- Completing a challenge unlocks badges displayed on your profile and leaderboard.
- Badges may unlock experimental features or beta invites.

---

## Leaderboard

- Shows top performers by points over the last 30 days.
- Tie-breakers favor the earliest completion time.

---

## Tips

- Start with one or two challenges to avoid overwhelm.
- Combine challenges with recommendations of similar scope for efficiency.
- Revisit completed challenges quarterly to maintain security posture.

---

## FAQ

- **Can I abandon a challenge?** Yes, click “Leave Challenge”; progress is
  saved but badge rewards disappear.
- **Do challenges expire?** Some time-boxed challenges show a countdown. Finish
  before the deadline to keep rewards.
