# Frequently Asked Questions

## General

**What is Trace Trail?**  
A privacy intelligence platform that aggregates your online footprint, scores
risk, and recommends actions to stay safe.

**How often should I run an analysis?**  
We recommend weekly runs or whenever you add new social accounts.

**Which platforms are supported?**  
Facebook, Instagram, LinkedIn, Twitter/X, plus more coming soon. Connect them
via the Connections page.

---

## Security & Privacy

**Do you store my passwords?**  
No. We only store hashed credentials and OAuth tokens required to fetch public
signals. Passwords never leave your device.

**Can I delete my data?**  
Yes. Contact support or use the in-app “Delete account” option (coming soon) to
remove all records within 30 days.

**Is my data shared?**  
Data is never sold. Limited information may be used to improve models, anonymized
and aggregated.

---

## Troubleshooting

**I can’t log in.**  
Use the Forgot Password flow. If issues persist, ensure cookies are enabled and
try a different browser.

**Scores look wrong.**  
Reconnect accounts or rerun analysis. If discrepancies remain, open a ticket
with the analysis timestamp and platform details.

**Recommendations repeat.**  
Some recommendations reappear until the system detects verified changes. Mark
them complete and rerun analysis to confirm.

## Billing (Future)

**Is Trace Trail free?**  
Currently yes. Paid tiers will introduce premium reports and enterprise
dashboards. Existing beta users receive advanced notice before pricing changes.

**How do I upgrade?**  
Once billing launches, go to Settings → Billing. For enterprise, contact
sales@tracetrail.com.
