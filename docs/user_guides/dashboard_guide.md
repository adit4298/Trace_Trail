# Dashboard Guide

Welcome to the Trace Trail dashboard. This guide helps end users interpret the
widgets and take action on privacy insights.

---

## Overview Tab

- **Risk Score Card** — Shows current score (0–100) with a trend indicator.
  - Green ≥80, Yellow 60–79, Red <60.
  - Click “View Analysis” to see full breakdown.
- **Quick Stats** — Tiles for Linked Accounts, Recommendations due, Completed
  Challenges, and Reports.
- **Recent Activity** — Timeline of key events (new recommendations, completed
  actions, syncs).

---

## Insights Tab

- **Risk Trend Chart** — Weekly score history. Hover to see exact values.
- **Platform Breakdown** — Compare exposure level per social network.
- **Heatmap** — Visualizes data leaks or public posts by day/time.

Tips:

- Use filters (top right) to focus on specific platforms or time ranges.
- Export CSV for custom analysis (coming soon).

---

## Recommendations Tab

- Cards show priority (High/Medium/Low), estimated impact, and steps.
- Use the “Mark Complete” button once you finish the action; the score updates
  on the next analysis run.
- Snooze low-priority tasks to declutter the list (feature flag).

---

## Challenges Tab

- Join gamified challenges to improve privacy hygiene.
- Progress bars fill as you complete recommended actions tied to the challenge.
- Earn badges (shown at top) that unlock in-app perks.

---

## Reports Tab

- Generate downloadable summaries (PDF/CSV).
- Each report includes current score, platform insights, and recommendation
  status.
- Click the timestamp to re-download; reports expire after 30 days.

---

## Troubleshooting

- **Data looks stale:** Press “Refresh data” button or run a new analysis.
- **Score won’t change:** Complete more recommendations or connect additional
  accounts so the engine has fresh data.
- **Missing platform:** Visit Connections tab and link the platform; it will
  appear after a sync completes.
